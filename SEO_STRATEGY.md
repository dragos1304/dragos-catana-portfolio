## Strategy
- Head terms ("creare site", "optimizare seo") are dominated by site builders and agencies. Win with niche plus intent: clinics, lawyers, local businesses, AI visibility.
- Pillar "Ce este SEO" leads to clusters: SEO local / Google Business Profile, recenzii Google, cât costă un site (factors only, no prices), ce trebuie să conțină site-ul unei clinici, site pentru avocați (check UNBR rules), mentenanță site, GEO.
- Needs your decision: an online-store page (creare magazin online 1300/mo) and a site-maintenance service (260/mo). City pages (București 480/mo) only with real local proof, to avoid doorway pages.
- Cadence: 2 articles a month. Track in Google Search Console.
