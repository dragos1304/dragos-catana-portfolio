# Client proposal template

**Version:** v1 · 2026-11-19
**Made with:** the `sales-enablement` skill (coreyhaines31/marketingskills, installed from the Skill Issue marketplace): its proposal structure, customization rules and objection-handling framework. Voice and claims follow `BRAND.md`.

**When to send:** after the free audit and the 30-minute call, within 24 hours of the call.
**Length:** 5–7 pages. Shorter is better.
**How to use:**
1. Copy everything between the two `---8<---` lines into a new document for the client (Google Docs, then export to PDF).
2. Replace every `[bracket]`. Delete every line that starts with `>` (those are notes for you).
3. Run the checklist at the end before sending.

The rules from the skill that matter most here:
- **Mirror their words.** Use the exact phrases the client said on the call.
- **Customize the first page at minimum.** A generic proposal signals low effort.
- **Don't bury the price.** Prices stay off the website, but in a private proposal the price is stated clearly in its own section.
- **Only claims you can defend.** The client can check everything you write.

## For you: likely questions and how to answer them

The `sales-enablement` skill's objection framework, adapted. Each answer uses only proof you can defend. Answer the real concern, then end with a question.

| What the client says | The real concern (likely) | Answer | Question to keep it moving |
|---|---|---|---|
| „E prea scump." | Not sure it pays back, or comparing with a cheaper offer that has a different scope | Agree the cost matters, then compare scope line by line: what's included here (SEO, AI, migration without losing Google) and what's often missing elsewhere. Offer to cut deliverables, not quality. | „Ce ar trebui să aducă site-ul ca investiția să merite pentru tine?" |
| „Altcineva mi-a cerut jumătate." | Price anchoring | Don't argue with the other number. Ask what it includes. | „Pot să văd ce intră în oferta lor, ca să comparăm același lucru?" |
| „Nu acum, poate la anul." | Busy, or doesn't feel the urgency | Respect it. Point to what the audit showed is lost meanwhile, without pressure. | „Vrei să-ți retestez întrebările din audit peste 3 luni, ca să vezi dacă s-a schimbat ceva?" |
| „Ești la început / ești student." | Risk: will he deliver and still be around? | Acknowledge it. Point to the measured Cheesy Media result, the step-by-step approvals and the maintenance option. | „Ce te-ar face să te simți sigur(ă) pe colaborare?" |
| „Garantezi că apar în ChatGPT?" | Wants certainty | No, and explain why honestly. Promise the before/after measurement instead. | „Dacă îți arăt diferența pe aceleași întrebări, ar fi suficient ca dovadă?" |
| „Trebuie să vorbesc cu partenerul / medicul-șef." | Not the only decider | Offer a 15-minute call with the other person, or a one-page summary for them. | „Ce ar vrea să afle el (ea) ca să decidă?" |

**When not to push:** if they need an online store, paid ads or something outside your four services, say so and point them elsewhere. Saying no to a bad fit protects the "Honest" perception in `BRAND.md`.
