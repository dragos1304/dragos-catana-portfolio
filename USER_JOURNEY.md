# User journey: from "nobody recommends me" to a call with Dragoș

Made 18 Nov 2026 with two skills:
- `design:user-research`: journey mapping and jobs to be done.
- `marketing:content-creation`: landing-page structure, story hook, one primary CTA, "you" language.

There is **no primary research yet**. Everything below comes from the decisions in `PORTFOLIO_STRUCTURE.md` §0 and is marked as an assumption where it guesses a feeling or a behaviour. Validate it with 5 short calls with real clinic, law-firm and local-business owners. An interview guide is at the end.

## Persona (assumption, one composite)
**Ana, 41, owns a small dental clinic in Cluj-Napoca.** She has 3 dentists and a receptionist. Her site was built in 2017 by a nephew. She is not technical, she is busy, and she decides on trust. She reads on her phone between patients. A law-firm partner or the owner of a local service business has the same shape: expert in their own field, short on time, wary of agencies.

**Job to be done:** "When new patients (clients) choose someone online, I want my practice to be the one they find and trust, so that the phone rings without me chasing it."

## The journey

| Stage | What Ana does | What she thinks / feels (assumption) | Touchpoint | What the site must show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Trigger | A new patient says: "I asked ChatGPT for a good clinic nearby, it named someone else." Or she notices her site looks old on her phone. | "Did I fall behind without noticing?" Unease, a little embarrassment. | Conversation, her own phone | A first screen that names that exact moment, in her words. |
| 2. Search | Googles "creare site clinică", "optimizare seo", or asks ChatGPT "cine face site-uri pentru clinici în Cluj". | "I don't want an agency that talks in jargon and disappears." Wary. | Google, AI assistants, a friend's recommendation | Plain Romanian, the field she is in (clinics, law firms, local services), a real name and city. |
| 3. Evaluate | Scrolls the homepage, opens "Despre", maybe the Cheesy Media case. | "Is this a real person? Will he understand my business? Is he too young?" Curious but guarded. | Homepage, About, case study | A person, not a logo: who Dragoș is, why he does this, how he works, a real measured result, an honest limit ("nobody can guarantee #1 in ChatGPT"). |
| 4. Decide | Considers the free audit. | "What do I lose? Will I get spammed or pressured?" Low trust in "free". | Audit page, form | Low risk: manual, free, 24 h, no obligation, short form, what happens next. |
| 5. Audit | Receives the audit by email. | "He actually looked at my clinic." Relief, if it is specific. | Email (Dragoș) | Specific findings in plain language, 3 priorities, an invitation to talk. |
| 6. Call | Books 30 minutes on Calendly. | "I'm talking to the person who will do the work." Confidence. | Calendly, video call | The same person, the same tone; a clear quote afterwards, no published price list. |
| 7. Work and measure | Redesign or SEO and AI visibility; progress shown per stage. | "I can see what changed." Trust becomes a referral. | Shared progress, monthly report | Before/after evidence, re-tested AI questions. |

## Moments that matter
1. **Recognition (stage 1).** If the hero does not mirror her trigger, she leaves. The hero states the outcome she wants and the person who delivers it.
2. **"Is he real?" (stage 3).** This is the biggest risk for a solo freelancer. Answer it with the personal story, a face as soon as a photo exists, a real quote from a professor, and a real result. Never with invented testimonials.
3. **The free-audit leap (stage 4).** It is the only primary CTA. Remove every reason to hesitate next to the button.

## How the site tells it (homepage chapters)
The homepage becomes one story in chapters, written as Ana's journey with Dragoș's part in each step:
1. **Hero:** the outcome plus the person ("Eu sunt Dragoș...").
2. **The moment:** "Un pacient îți spune că ChatGPT a recomandat pe altcineva."
3. **The journey in 5 steps,** told stage by stage: audit, plan, build, measure, recommended. Each step has "what you do" and "what I do".
4. **Services,** for whom.
5. **Proof:** the Cheesy Media case, with real Lighthouse numbers.
6. **A letter from Dragoș:** personal, first person, with the professor quote.
7. **FAQ:** honest limits.
8. **Footer CTA:** the free audit.

## Interview guide (to validate this map)
1. Warm-up: tell me about your practice and how new clients find you today.
2. Context: the last time you changed or thought about your website, what triggered it?
3. Deep dive: have you ever asked ChatGPT or Google for a business like yours? What came up?
4. Deep dive: what would make you trust or distrust a freelancer for this?
5. Reaction: show the homepage. What would you do next? What stops you?
6. Wrap-up: what did we not ask that matters?
