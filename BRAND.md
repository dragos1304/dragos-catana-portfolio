## 5. How he interacts with clients
*(Evidence: the process on the homepage and the way Dragoș works in this project. Intended where noted.)*

1. **Listens first.** Free audit and a 30-minute call before any offer. He doesn't pitch before he knows the business.
2. **Writes, doesn't recite.** The audit is written by him, manually, in plain Romanian, with three priorities.
3. **Shows each step.** The client sees progress per stage and approves what matters: texts, pages, images.
4. **Asks before acting.** No purchase, plan change or irreversible step without the client's yes.
5. **Measures in front of the client.** Same Google and ChatGPT questions before and after, so the client sees the difference.
6. **Stays after launch.** Maintenance, small edits and a short report in plain words *(intended: offered as a service, no long-term client yet)*.
7. **Decides fast, explains briefly.** Short messages, one question at a time, a recommendation instead of a menu *(evidence: how Dragoș writes in this project)*.

## 6. Brand voice
Built from live site copy, as the skill requires. There's no performance data on copy yet, so these are the attributes visible in the text, not ones proven to convert.

| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Real line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | First person, his name, his reasons | Self-centred; "I" in every sentence | „Lucrez singur, cu bună știință." |
| **Plain** | Short sentences, everyday Romanian, one idea per line | Simplistic or vague | „Îți spun ce merge, ce lipsește și cele trei lucruri care contează cel mai mult." |
| **Honest** | States limits next to promises | Negative or hedging everything | „Nu, și nimeni nu poate. Rezultatele variază." |
| **Calm and sure** | Clear recommendations, no hype | Cold or arrogant | „Fără surprize și fără intermediari." |

**Tone by context**
| Context | Turn up | Turn down | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website | Clarity, the client's outcome | Technical terms | The first screen says the client's problem in their words. |
| Audit and proposal | Specifics about *their* business | Generic benefits | If it would read the same for another client, rewrite it. |
| Messages and calls | Warmth, speed, one clear next step | Long explanations | Answer first, explain after. |
| Problems and delays | Ownership, the fix, the new date | Excuses | If it's his fault, say so in the first line. |
| LinkedIn and social | Opinions, lessons from real work | Corporate tone | Still plain and honest; never a claim the site can't back. |

**Words**
| List | Words |
|---|---|
| **Terms we own** | audit gratuit (manual, 24 h), vizibilitate în AI, „recomandat de ChatGPT", înainte → după, un singur om |
| **Client words** *(to validate)* | „nu mă găsește nimeni", „site-ul arată vechi", „pacienții m-au întrebat", „vreau să sune telefonul" |
| **Never use** (and why) | *agenție* (he isn't one), *garantat locul 1* (nobody can), *revoluționar / de ultimă generație* (hype breaks honesty), invented stats (breaks trust and his rule), *soluții integrate* (empty agency phrase) |

   - „Cum ai descrie colaborarea unui prieten?"
   - „Ce ar fi trebuit să fac mai bine?"
2. Ask Cheesy Media for a short quote and permission to publish it.
3. Run the 5 interviews in `USER_JOURNEY.md` with clinic, law-firm and local-business owners.
4. Move confirmed phrases into **Client words** and change labels from *to validate* to *evidence*. Add a changelog line each time.
